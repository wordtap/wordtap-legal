# Privacy Policy

<p class="meta">WordTap app · version of 24 October 2026</p>

## 1. Who processes your data

1.1. The controller of personal data of users of the WordTap app (the "App") is ANDRII MELNYCHUK, an individual, Ukraine ("we", "us"). Our contact details are at the end of this document.

1.2. We process data in accordance with the laws of Ukraine on personal data protection and, for users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, also with regard to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## 2. What data we process and why

| Data | Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Email address and user ID provided by Apple or Google when you sign in (with Sign in with Apple the address may be hidden) | Creating an account and signing in | Performance of a contract (the Terms of Use) |
| Anonymous account identifier, if you use the App without signing in | Same | Performance of a contract |
| Name and avatar, if you provide them | Showing your profile | Performance of a contract |
| Your words, translations, notes, photos attached to words, levels, statistics, streak, course progress, settings | Storing and syncing your data between devices | Performance of a contract |
| Original transaction ID, product ID and subscription expiry date | Checking Premium on all your devices | Performance of a contract; our legitimate interest in preventing abuse |
| A word or phrase you look up in the dictionary | Showing the translation and meaning | Performance of a contract |
| The word you tapped and the sentence from the article | Explaining the meaning of the word in that sentence | Performance of a contract |
| Technical request data (IP address, time, device type) | Operating and securing the servers | Legitimate interest in the security of the service |

2.1. We do not receive your payment details. Payments are handled by Apple. We only see the subscription status listed in the table.

2.2. We do not collect data for advertising, do not use advertising identifiers, do not track you across other apps or websites, do not use analytics services and do not collect crash reports. We do not ask for access to your location, contacts, calendar or photo library. You choose the photos you attach to words yourself.

2.3. The App does not show ads and we do not sell data.

## 3. Speech recognition and microphone

3.1. For the Speak It game the App asks for access to the microphone and to speech recognition. Audio is processed by iOS. Recognition may run on the device, and when a network connection is available your speech may be sent to Apple and processed under Apple's rules. We do not receive or store speech recordings.

## 4. Notifications

4.1. Reminders and notifications about new articles are created by the App itself on your device (local notifications). They require your permission in the system. Notifications about news are off by default and can be turned on and off in the App settings. We do not collect push notification tokens.

## 5. Who receives data

5.1. To operate the App we use third-party providers:

| Recipient | What it receives | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase (hosting in the Western Europe region, Ireland) | Account data, words, photos, progress, subscription status, technical request data | Data storage and server operation |
| Apple | Data for Sign in with Apple, payments and subscriptions via the App Store; speech recognition (see section 3) | Sign-in, payments, recognition |
| Google | Data for Sign in with Google | Sign-in |
| Anthropic (USA) | The word and the sentence from the article; not your name, email address or user ID | Explaining the meaning of a word |
| dictionaryapi.dev, MyMemory (Translated) | The word or phrase you look up, not linked to your account | Dictionary and translation |

5.2. We do not disclose data to anyone else, except where the law requires it.

5.3. Some providers are located outside the EEA (in particular in the USA). Data is transferred to them on terms that ensure protection in accordance with applicable law, including standard contractual clauses where they apply.

## 6. How long we keep data

6.1. Account data is kept as long as the account exists. After you delete the account, we delete your profile, words, photos, levels, statistics and subscription record from the server. Data may remain in the hosting provider's backups for a short time until the backups are overwritten.

6.2. Data stored only on your device is removed when you delete the App.

6.3. Server technical logs are kept for the period set by the hosting provider.

## 7. Your rights

7.1. You may: obtain information about your data and a copy of it; have inaccurate data corrected; have your data erased; restrict processing or object to it; receive your data in a portable format; withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

7.2. You can delete your account and all data on the server in the App: Profile → Settings → Delete account. This works for all accounts, including anonymous ones. Deletion is irreversible.

7.3. For anything else, write to the address at the end of this document. We will reply within 30 days.

7.4. If you believe we violate your rights, you may lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country. In Ukraine this is the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights.

## 8. Security

8.1. Data is transmitted over a secure connection (HTTPS). Access to the database is restricted by rules under which each user can see only their own records. It is not possible to exclude completely the risk of a leak when data is transmitted and stored on the internet.

## 9. Children

9.1. The App is not intended for children under 13, and in EEA countries where the age of digital consent is higher, for children under 16 without parental consent. We do not knowingly collect data of such children. If you learn that a child has given us data, tell us and we will delete it.

## 10. Changes to this policy

10.1. We may change this policy. The current version is always published at this address, and its date is shown at the top. We will additionally notify you in the App of material changes.
